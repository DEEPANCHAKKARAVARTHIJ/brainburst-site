Website privacy

Last updated: November 2026

This notice describes the BrainBurst informational website and its on-page mini-challenge. It does not describe any separate game apps or services linked from this site.

Data you enter

This website does not provide accounts, registration forms, purchases, or newsletter signups. The mini-challenge operates in your browser and does not intentionally send your answers or score to a BrainBurst server.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, GitHub may process technical request data, including IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the site. Consult GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Cookies and analytics

We have not added analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or first-party cookies to this website. Third-party sites you choose to open may have their own practices.

Contact

For site feedback, visit the developer's GitHub profile. Contact details and policies for individual games, when applicable, should be reviewed separately.

This notice should be reviewed and updated if you add analytics, advertising, forms, authentication, or other data collection.
